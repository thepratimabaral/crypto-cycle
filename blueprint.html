Project plan · v1 · October 2026

Crypto Cycle: full history, a cycle score, and predictions we can check

A web dashboard for BTC and the top coins. It shows each coin's complete price history, a 0–100 cycle score like KŌINI's, a forecast for 30, 90 and 365 days, and a public record of whether those forecasts came true.

01 · Goal

What we're building

"What has BTC done?"

Whole history

Daily price since July 2010 for BTC, and since each coin was listed for the others, on a log chart with the cycle score underneath.

"Where are we now?"

Cycle score + forecast

One 0–100 score built from valuation and trend indicators, plus the chance that the price is higher in 30, 90 and 365 days.

"Can I trust it?"

Proof

A backtest over every past day, a comparison against simple baseline rules, and a live log of predictions that get scored automatically when they come due.

Coins in v1: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, ADA, DOGE.

02 · Architecture

How it fits together

A Python pipeline runs once a day. It downloads data, calculates everything, and writes plain JSON files. The website is static: it only reads those files. There's no server or database to run, so hosting is free.

05 · Indicators

What goes into the score

The indicators fall into two groups, like KŌINI. Valuation asks whether the price is cheap or expensive compared with its own history. Trend asks which way the market is moving and how strongly.

06 · Scoring

Turning indicators into one 0–100 score

Raw indicators use different units (a ratio, a %, an RSI). So each day, every raw value is converted to a percentile against that coin's own past: 0 means the lowest reading ever up to that day, and 100 means the highest. It only compares with past days, never future ones. That rule is what makes the backtest fair.

indicator score = percentile of today's value among all values up to today (needs 365 days first) valuation = average of valuation indicator scores (needs at least 2) trend = average of trend indicator scores (needs at least 2) cycle score = (valuation + trend) ÷ 2
Capitulation0–20
Accumulation20–40
Early Expansion40–60
Mid Bull60–80
Euphoria80–100

Like KŌINI, we'll flag days when valuation and trend disagree by more than 25 points.

07 · Prediction

How a prediction is made

For each horizon (30, 90 and 365 days) we ask: on past days with a similar score, how often was the price higher afterwards?

1. Put today's score into a bucket of 10 points (e.g. 58 → bucket 50–60). 2. Take every past day in that bucket whose outcome is already known. 3. Count how often the price was higher after N days. 4. Pull that rate toward the overall up-rate (20 pseudo-days), so small buckets don't over-claim. P(up) = (ups in bucket + 20 × overall up-rate) ÷ (days in bucket + 20)

Example of what the dashboard will show (numbers made up): "BTC score 58. Higher in 90 days: 64%, based on 1,240 similar past days. Median 90-day return on those days: +18%."

This method is easy to explain and hard to overfit. Fancier models such as machine learning can be added later, but only if they beat this one in the same backtest.

08 · Validation

How we find out if it works

Walk-forward backtest

  • Replay history one day at a time.
  • Each day's prediction uses only data that was available on that day.
  • Score every prediction against what really happened.
  • BTC gives us about 13 years of test days. Newer coins give fewer.

Baselines it must beat

  • Coin flip: always 50%.
  • Always up: the overall up-rate so far. Hard to beat for BTC.
  • 200-day rule: up if price is above its 200-day average.
  • Valuation only and Trend only: shows which half does the work.

Live prediction log

  • Every daily run saves that day's predictions. They're never edited afterwards.
  • When a prediction's date arrives, it is scored automatically as ✓ or ✗.
  • The log is committed to git, so the timestamps can be checked.
  • It starts empty. The first 30-day results arrive 30 days after launch.
MetricWhat it meansGood result
Hit rateHow often the up/down call was rightHigher than the "always up" baseline
Brier scoreError of the % itself (0 is perfect, 0.25 is a coin flip)Lower than the baselines
Brier skill% improvement over "always up"Above 0%
CalibrationWhen it says 70%, does it happen about 70% of the time?Points close to the diagonal
Return spreadAverage return after "up" calls minus after "down" callsClearly positive

Honest caveat for the dashboard: consecutive days share most of their future window, so 4,000 test days of 365-day predictions are really only about 11 independent years.

12 · Risks

Limits & risks

Few cycles

BTC has had only about 4 full bull/bear cycles. Any cycle model learns from a small sample, so results can look better than they really are.

Short alt histories

SOL and DOGE have only a few years of data, so their forecasts get lower confidence labels.

Free API changes

Free APIs can add limits at any time, as CoinGecko and CryptoCompare already have. The cache keeps the site running, and sources can be swapped.

Not financial advice

The site shows probabilities from history, not guarantees. The disclaimer appears on every page.
